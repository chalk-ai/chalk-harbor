# Cost of service (internal)

Every action has a cost to Larkspur. Use them when policy gives you a choice; never let them
override a remedy the policy requires.

| Action | Approximate cost to Larkspur |
| --- | --- |
| Refund or credit | the amount issued |
| Out-of-window exchange exception | about 30% of the item price (reverse logistics + refurbishment loss) |
| Technician visit | $145 (`emergency` $260, `haul_away_pickup` $95) |
| Escalation to a human | $25 (`urgent` $40) |
| Follow-up reminder | $4 |
