# Installation workmanship warranty

Larkspur warrants its **installation work for 1 year from the installation date**, at no charge.

Covered (dispatch category `install_warranty`):
- leaks at water supply or drain connections Larkspur made;
- gas connection problems at the connector Larkspur installed;
- dryer venting Larkspur installed, leveling, anti-tip brackets, door alignment caused by install;
- electrical cords/plugs Larkspur attached.

Not covered: failures inside the appliance (that is a product defect — KB-106), connections made by
someone else, and damage from later changes by the customer.

More than 1 year after installation, workmanship issues are a **paid service call** (KB-106).
If a workmanship problem caused damage to the home, also follow KB-103 "Damage to the home".
If a workmanship problem is a safety hazard, follow KB-108 first.
