# Product defects, manufacturer warranty and paid service calls

- **Within 30 days of delivery:** defective items are exchanged or returned for free in the portal
  (KB-101, KB-102).
- **After 30 days:** repairs are covered by the **manufacturer's warranty** (typically 1 year parts
  and labor from delivery), or by ProtectPlan if the customer bought it (KB-107).

Manufacturer warranty service is booked **by the customer** directly with the brand:

| Brand | Products | Warranty service line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Norvik | washers, dryers, dishwashers | 1-800-555-0141 · norvik.example/service |
| Calloway | refrigerators, freezers | 1-800-555-0177 · calloway.example/care |
| Brisa | microwaves, ranges | 1-800-555-0123 · brisa.example/support |
| Tamsin | ranges, wall ovens, cooktops | 1-800-555-0190 · tamsin.example/warranty |

**Paid service call.** For customers without coverage (or who prefer Larkspur), a Larkspur
technician visit costs **$129** (diagnosis + first 30 minutes of labor; parts quoted on site). Only
dispatch one (category `paid_service_call`) after the customer has **explicitly agreed to the $129
fee**. Never waive it.
