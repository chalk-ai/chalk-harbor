# ProtectPlan extended protection

ProtectPlan is Larkspur's extended protection plan, sold per appliance for 3 or 5 years from
delivery. The order record shows whether an item has ProtectPlan and when it expires.

- Covers mechanical and electrical failures from normal use, parts and labor, **no service fee**.
- Book a repair visit with dispatch category `protect_plan_repair`.
- **Food-loss reimbursement** (refrigerators/freezers, up to $150 per incident) is filed **by the
  customer** at the ProtectPlan portal with receipts. Agents do **not** issue it with the refund tool.
- ProtectPlan does not cover cosmetic damage, misuse, or items without an active plan.
