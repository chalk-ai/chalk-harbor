# Damage found at or after delivery

## Reported within 48 hours of delivery
The customer chooses one of:
- **Free replacement** — started in the portal Returns Center with reason "arrived damaged"; no fees.
- **Keep it with a discount** (agent issues a refund with reason `damage_discount`):
  - cosmetic damage that is **not visible** once installed (side panel against a cabinet, back,
    underside): **10% of the item price**;
  - cosmetic damage that **is visible** once installed (front, door, top, control panel):
    **20% of the item price**.
  The discount is a fixed percentage, not a negotiation, and it counts toward the agent's
  refund authority (KB-111). If the discount is above your authority, escalate it for approval
  instead of issuing part of it.

## Reported after 48 hours
- **Cosmetic** damage is **not eligible** for a discount or replacement unless the proof-of-delivery
  (POD) notes recorded damage at drop-off.
- **Functional** problems (doesn't cool, leaks, won't start) are defects: within 30 days the customer
  can exchange in the portal; after that see KB-102 / KB-106.

## Damage to the home
Damage to floors, walls, cabinets or other property caused by delivery or installation is a
**claim**: escalate to the `claims` queue (KB-112). Agents do not settle or estimate claims and do not
admit fault; tell the customer a claims specialist will contact them within 3 business days.
