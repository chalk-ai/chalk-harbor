# Fraud and abuse signals

Signals (shown in the customer profile and order record):
- **3 or more refunds/credits in the last 180 days**;
- a **chargeback** on any past order;
- a claim that **contradicts the proof of delivery** (POD photo, GPS stamp, signature), e.g.
  "never delivered" when the POD shows the item placed in the home and signed for.

When a request carries **two or more** signals:
- do **not** issue refunds or credits, exceptions or dispatches for that request;
- escalate to the `risk` queue (priority normal) with the signals you saw;
- stay courteous and **do not accuse the customer** or mention fraud/risk; say the request needs a
  specialist review and that they will hear back within 3 business days;
- schedule a follow-up (KB-113).

A single signal on its own is not enough to withhold a remedy the policy clearly gives.
