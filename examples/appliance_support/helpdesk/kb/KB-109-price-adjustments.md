# Price adjustments and price matching

- If **Larkspur lowers its own price** on the same SKU **within 14 days of the order date**, refund
  the difference once per item (reason `price_adjustment`). Compare against the current Larkspur
  price shown in the order record.
- After 14 days: no adjustment.
- **We do not match competitor prices**, including other retailers' sales, marketplace listings, or
  manufacturer rebates. Do not offer goodwill to make up a competitor's price.
- Open-box and clearance purchases are final price.
