# Exchanges and out-of-window exchange exceptions

## Standard exchanges (within 30 days of delivery)
Customers exchange items themselves in the portal's Returns Center (KB-122). Defective items are
exchanged at no cost; other exchanges follow the fees in KB-101. **No agent action or exception is
needed inside the 30-day window** — do not use the exception tool for in-window exchanges.

## Out-of-window exchange exception (agent tool: `approve_exchange_exception`)
An exception lets a customer exchange an item **after** the 30-day window. It is expensive and is
granted only when **all** of the following are true:

1. The item is **defective** — it does not function as designed. Cosmetic issues reported after 48
   hours, colour/size/style preference, and buyer's remorse never qualify.
2. The item was delivered **no more than 75 days ago** (i.e. at most 45 days past the window).
3. **Either** the customer is **Gold or Platinum** tier, **or** Larkspur itself had a service
   failure on the same order (a missed delivery window, an incomplete or faulty installation).
4. The customer has **not** received an out-of-window exception in the **last 12 months**.

If any condition fails, do not grant the exception. Offer the right alternative instead:
- ProtectPlan holders → ProtectPlan repair (KB-107);
- otherwise → the manufacturer's warranty (KB-106), and optionally a paid service call.

Only one exception per item. Record in the reason which conditions you verified.
