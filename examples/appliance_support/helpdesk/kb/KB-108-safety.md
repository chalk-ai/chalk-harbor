# Safety emergencies — gas, electrical, water

**Safety comes before anything else in the conversation.** Your first message must give the safety
instructions below. Do not troubleshoot a hazard, and do not ask diagnostic questions first.

| Hazard | Tell the customer, immediately |
| --- | --- |
| Gas smell near a gas appliance | Leave the home now with everyone (and pets). Do not use light switches, appliances, phones or flames inside. From outside, call the gas utility's emergency line or **911**. Do not go back in until cleared. |
| Burning smell, smoke, sparks, a hot cord or outlet | If it is safe, unplug the appliance or switch off its circuit breaker. If there is smoke or fire, leave and call **911**. |
| Active water leak / flooding | Turn off the water supply valve to the appliance (or the home's main valve). Keep away from wet outlets. |

Then, every time:
1. **Escalate** to the `safety` queue with priority **urgent** (KB-112).
2. If the hazard involves something Larkspur installed or delivered in the last year, **dispatch an
   `emergency` technician for today** (same-day emergency slots are always available). Otherwise
   direct the customer to the utility / a licensed professional.
3. Schedule a follow-up for the next business day (KB-113).
