# Backorders and delivery delays

- The order record shows the originally promised delivery date and the current ETA.
- If the item arrives **more than 7 days after the originally promised date**, the agent may issue a
  one-time **$25 goodwill credit** per order (reason `goodwill`).
- Customers can cancel a backordered item for a full refund in the portal at any time before it ships.
- Schedule a follow-up for the business day after the ETA (KB-113) so we can confirm delivery.
