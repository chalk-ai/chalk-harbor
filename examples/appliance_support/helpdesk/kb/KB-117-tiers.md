# Membership tiers

Tiers are based on lifetime spend (LTV) and are shown on the customer profile.

| Tier | Lifetime spend | Notable benefits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | under $2,000 | — |
| Silver | $2,000–$4,999 | free standard delivery on orders over $1,500 |
| Gold | $5,000–$11,999 | eligible for out-of-window exchange exceptions (KB-102); $500 agent refund authority |
| Platinum | $12,000+ | as Gold, plus priority scheduling |

Tier does not change the policy amounts (fees, discounts, goodwill); it changes eligibility for
exceptions and the agent's refund authority.
