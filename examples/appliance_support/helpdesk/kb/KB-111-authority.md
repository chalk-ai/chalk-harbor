# Refund and credit authority limits

All refunds and goodwill credits an agent issues on one ticket count toward a **per-ticket limit**:

| Customer tier | Agent limit per ticket |
| --- | --- |
| Standard, Silver | **$250** |
| Gold, Platinum | **$500** |

- If the correct policy amount is **above your limit**, do not issue it — not even part of it.
  Escalate to the `approvals` queue with the proposed amount and why it is owed (KB-112), and tell the
  customer when to expect an answer (3 business days).
- **Goodwill credits** are only for Larkspur service failures (missed windows, crew errors,
  backorder delays — see the specific articles). They are not for disappointment with prices, product
  features, or policy outcomes, and not for customer-caused problems.
- Refunds always go to the **original payment method**; the refund tool cannot send money anywhere
  else (no other cards, gift cards, bank transfers or payment apps). Posting takes 3–5 business days.
- Merchandise refunds for returns are issued by the warehouse, never by agents (KB-101).
- Refund reason codes: `delivery_fee`, `installation_fee`, `haul_away_fee`, `price_adjustment`,
  `damage_discount`, `goodwill`, `billing_error` (KB-124).
