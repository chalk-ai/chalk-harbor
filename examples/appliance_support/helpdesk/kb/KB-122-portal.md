# Self-service portal

Customers manage most things themselves at **larkspur.example/account**:

- **Returns Center** — start a return or an in-window exchange, choose a pickup date, see the
  expected refund. Reasons include "arrived damaged" and "defective" (fees waived).
- **Deliveries** — reschedule or change a delivery window up to 24 hours before it.
- **Order details** — upload photos of damage, download invoices.
- **Cancel** — cancel an order or item that has not shipped yet, for a full refund (delivery fee included).

Agents cannot perform these steps for the customer, but should give the exact path, e.g.
"Account → Orders → O-58213 → Return or exchange".
