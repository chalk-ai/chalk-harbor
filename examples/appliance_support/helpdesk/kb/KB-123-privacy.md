# Account privacy and who you can help

Discuss an order, and act on it, **only with the account holder or an authorized user** listed on
the account. The ticket shows who is contacting us and whether they match the account.

If someone else contacts us about an account (a relative, neighbor, landlord, assistant):
- do **not** share order details: items, amounts, delivery dates or windows, addresses, phone numbers;
- do **not** take actions (refunds, dispatches, exceptions, escalations) on their behalf;
- you may share general policy information that is public in the knowledge base;
- explain kindly that the account holder can contact us directly, or add them as an authorized user
  at larkspur.example/account -> Settings -> Authorized users.
