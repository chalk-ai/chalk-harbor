# Returns policy

**Return window.** Customers may return merchandise within **30 days of delivery**.

| Condition of the item | Window | Restocking fee | Return pickup fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unopened / not installed | 30 days from delivery | none | $79 (waived if defective) |
| Installed, **defective** | 30 days from delivery | none | waived |
| Installed, **not defective** (changed mind, noise within spec, look) | **15 days** from delivery | **15% of item price** | $79 |

- Installation labor fees and haul-away fees are **not refundable** on a non-defective return.
- The merchandise refund is calculated as: item price − restocking fee − pickup fee (when they apply).
- Customers start every return themselves in the Returns Center of the self-service portal (KB-122).
  The warehouse issues the **merchandise refund automatically** once the pickup crew collects the item.
- **Agents never issue merchandise refunds with the refund tool.** Tell the customer the expected
  amount and that it posts to the original payment method 3–5 business days after pickup.
- Past the windows above, a non-defective item cannot be returned. Defective items past 30 days go
  through warranty (KB-106) or, rarely, an out-of-window exception (KB-102).
