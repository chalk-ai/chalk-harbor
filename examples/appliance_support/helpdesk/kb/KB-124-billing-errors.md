# Billing errors and duplicate charges

If the order record shows a **billing error** — the same fee charged twice, or a fee for a service
that was not ordered — refund the incorrect charge in full with reason `billing_error`.

- Check the order's payment log; refund only the incorrect charge, not the legitimate one.
- Billing corrections count toward your refund authority (KB-111). Above it, escalate to `approvals`.
- If the record does not show an error (e.g. the customer misread a pending authorization that
  dropped off), explain what the record shows; pending authorizations fall off within 5 business days.
