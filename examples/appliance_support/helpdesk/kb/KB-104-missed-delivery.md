# Missed and late delivery windows

A delivery window is **missed by Larkspur** when the crew does not arrive during the confirmed
4-hour window (or arrives more than 30 minutes after it ends) **without calling at least 2 hours
ahead**. Check the order's delivery events and driver notes.

**Remedy for a Larkspur miss**
- **First miss on an order:** refund the order's **delivery fee in full** (reason `delivery_fee`).
- **Each additional miss on the same order:** a **$50 goodwill credit** (reason `goodwill`),
  plus the delivery fee if it has not already been refunded. A delivery fee is refunded at most once
  per order — check the contact history for earlier refunds.

**Customer-caused misses** (nobody home after the driver called, no access, wrong address given):
no refund and no credit. The first reschedule is free.

Customers reschedule themselves in the portal (KB-122); agents cannot change delivery slots.
