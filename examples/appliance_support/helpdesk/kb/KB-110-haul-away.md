# Old appliance haul-away

Haul-away of an old appliance costs **$35 per unit** and must be purchased with the order.

If the customer paid for haul-away and the crew **did not take** the old unit:
1. Refund the haul-away fee for that unit (reason `haul_away_fee`).
2. Dispatch a free pickup crew (dispatch category `haul_away_pickup`) on a day the customer is
   available, within the next 7 days.

No follow-up reminder is needed for a pickup. If haul-away was not purchased, the customer can add it
in the portal for a future visit ($35 + $59 trip charge).
