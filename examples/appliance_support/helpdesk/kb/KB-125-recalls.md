# Manufacturer safety recalls

Recalls are run by the manufacturer. Current recalls on items Larkspur sells:

| Recall | Products | Hazard | Remedy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BR-2026-03 (Brisa) | BM-30 over-the-range microwaves made before May 2026 | overheating control board | stop using and unplug; free in-home repair booked at brisa.example/recall or 1-800-555-0124 |
| NV-2026-01 (Norvik) | ND-24 dishwashers made before Feb 2026 | door latch can cut fingers | free latch kit mailed after registering at norvik.example/recall |

- Tell the customer how to check whether their unit is affected (model and serial on the label) and
  give the manufacturer's recall link and number. Advise them to stop using the unit if the recall
  says so.
- Larkspur does not dispatch technicians or issue refunds for recalls, and recalls are not grounds
  for an out-of-window exchange.
- If the unit is smoking, sparking or burning **now**, it is a safety emergency: follow KB-108.
